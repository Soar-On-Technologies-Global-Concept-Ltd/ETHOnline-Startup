**Intentra --- Business Model & Go-To-Market**

*Intentra*

Version: September 2026

# 1. Business Model

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Revenue Stream                      Model
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Transaction fee                     Percentage or fixed fee on
                                      successful transactions

  Professional subscription           Enhanced profile, leads, analytics
                                      and tools

  Merchant subscription               Catalog, automation and commerce
                                      tools

  B2B procurement                     Subscription plus
                                      transaction/service fees

  Transaction Assurance API           Usage-based or tiered pricing

  Trust API                           Usage-based reputation/verification
                                      data

  Enterprise                          Contract pricing
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Initial Customer Acquisition

-   Recruit trusted service professionals in one Nigerian city/market
    first.

-   Use high-frequency service categories.

-   Partner with communities and local businesses.

-   Use referral incentives.

-   Demonstrate transactions rather than only marketing the technology.

# 3. Supply Strategy

Start with a narrow provider set and quality controls. A small trusted
network is more valuable than a large unverified directory.

# 4. Demand Strategy

-   Natural-language service requests

-   Referral campaigns

-   Community partnerships

-   Business procurement pilots

-   Content demonstrating transaction protection and accountability

# 5. Competitive Position

Do not compete as a generic marketplace, payment processor, MCP
protocol, wallet or blockchain product. Position Intentra around
transaction assurance: binding intent to authorization, agreement,
fulfillment evidence, outcome and resolution.

# 6. Moat

-   Intent-to-outcome transaction records

-   Agreement and fulfillment evidence

-   Resolution infrastructure

-   Provider performance history

-   Transaction reputation

-   African commerce network

-   Reusable assurance APIs

# 7. Geographic Expansion

Nigeria first. Expand only after proving provider quality, transaction
reliability and resolution operations. Potential later markets include
Ghana, Kenya and South Africa.
