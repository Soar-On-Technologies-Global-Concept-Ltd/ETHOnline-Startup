**Intentra --- Trust, Disputes & Operations**

*Intentra*

Version: September 2026

# 1. Trust Model

Trust is based on observed transaction performance rather than stars
alone.

-   Completed jobs

-   Completion rate

-   Cancellation rate

-   Response time

-   Dispute frequency

-   Refund frequency

-   Repeat customers

-   Verification status

-   Fulfillment evidence

-   Tenure

# 2. Provider Onboarding

1.  Create profile.

2.  Verify identity/business information where required.

3.  Add services, pricing and availability.

4.  Upload portfolio/evidence.

5.  Accept platform policies.

6.  Become eligible for relevant transactions.

# 3. Complaints

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Party                               Examples
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Customer                            No-show, incomplete work, wrong
                                      item, damage, incorrect charge,
                                      payment issue

  Provider                            Customer unavailable, scope change,
                                      refusal to accept delivery,
                                      incorrect complaint, payment issue
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Resolution Ladder

7.  Deterministic policy check

8.  AI-assisted evidence analysis

9.  GenLayer adjudication for selected ambiguous cases

10. Human review

# 5. Refund Outcomes

-   Full refund

-   Partial refund

-   No refund

-   Provider remediation/rework

-   Escalation

# 6. Fairness

Intentra must preserve evidence from both sides and avoid treating a
customer complaint as automatically true. Provider evidence and customer
evidence should be evaluated against the original agreement.

# 7. Operational KPIs

-   Resolution time

-   False-resolution rate

-   Refund accuracy

-   Provider response rate

-   Repeat complaints

-   Fraud rate

-   Human escalation rate
